     asked. Nothing moves and nothing is deleted.
  2. Put back your own storage/orders.json — the ZIP contains no order history.
     The catalog in this ZIP is your 1041-product spreadsheet of 8 September and
     is meant to replace the one on the server. Only put your own
     storage/catalog.json back if you have edited products SINCE exporting it.
  3. Delete the ZIP from the server.
  4. Check: open the site logged out and scroll past the passcode box. Then sign
     in, search for "xo", and press Share.

WHAT THE ORDER LOG STORES
- Reference, date, status, every line with SKU, variant, quantity, unit price and
  line total, the piece count, the order total, and the CUSTOMER'S NAME.
- It does NOT store the phone number, the business name, or the order notes.
  Those are typed into the cart, travel in the WhatsApp message, and never reach
  the server. Export CSV exports the same fields, name included.

SPREADSHEET IMPORT
- Export CSV from the dashboard, edit in Excel, and import the same file back.
- Import updates products by their product_id and adds any new rows. It does not
  delete: products you remove from the spreadsheet stay in the catalog until you
  delete them in the dashboard.

BULK PRODUCT PICTURES
- Name each main picture exactly as its SKU: DR-00001.jpg
- Add gallery pictures with a number: DR-00001-2.jpg, DR-00001-3.webp
- JPG, PNG and WEBP only, maximum 5 MB each. Upload in batches if your host
  limits one selection to 20 files.

SECURITY
- The customer passcode is the only thing between the public and your prices. The
  landing page shows categories, brands and counts — never a price.
- Five wrong password attempts from one connection locks logins for 15 minutes.
- TURNING ON TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION IS STRONGLY RECOMMENDED —
  Account & store > two-factor.

NOTE ON PRICE TIERS
A single cart line is capped at 999 pieces, so a tier whose minimum quantity is
1000 or more can never be reached. Keep tier minimums below 1000.
