DR PHONE WHOLESALE — INSTALLATION (v19)

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS VERSION

FASTER SCROLLING THROUGH LONG LISTS
You reported that products take a while to appear when you scroll, and that the
site feels a little slow. Three separate things were causing it. All three are
fixed. Measured on a mid-range phone, searching a common word that matches 231
of your products:

                                       before      after
  from typing to seeing products       1.1 sec    0.5 sec
  page built in one go                 358 KB      96 KB
  stutters during a 2-second scroll        4           1

1. The shop was building EVERY matching product at once. A search for a common
   word matches hundreds, and the phone froze for about a second while it built
   them, then showed them all together. It now draws the first 60 and adds more
   as you scroll — the same way the dashboard has always handled your 1000+
   products. The item count at the top still shows the true total.

2. Products faded in only after they were already well onto the screen, over
   about half a second. If you stopped scrolling at the wrong moment, a product
   that was on screen stayed invisible until you scrolled again — which is
   exactly the "takes a while to load" you were seeing. Products now fade in
   before they reach the screen, so they are already there when you arrive.

3. Every product card was asking the phone's graphics memory to reserve space
   for it, including hundreds far off screen. That is now only done where it is
   needed.

Nothing about the look changes. The fade-in is still there on the first screen.

ALSO IN THIS VERSION (from the v18 receipt update)
Every WhatsApp share now creates its own receipt. Previously a customer who
ordered twice from the same phone left only one receipt in the dashboard,
because the second order reused the first one's reference and replaced it.

INSTALLING THIS UPDATE
  1. Upload this ZIP into public_html and extract it there, overwriting when
     asked. Nothing moves and nothing is deleted.
  2. Put back your own storage/orders.json — the ZIP contains no order history.
     The catalog in this ZIP is your 1049-product spreadsheet; only put your own
     storage/catalog.json back if you have edited products since exporting it.
  3. Delete the ZIP from the server.
  4. Open the shop on your phone, search for something common like "cable", and
     scroll: products should appear as you reach them, with no waiting.

You do not have to sign in again, and no addresses change.

PICTURES
This ZIP carries 1048 pictures for 1049 products. One product, DR-000283, uses a
picture uploaded on the server itself, so it is not in the ZIP — extract OVER the
existing uploads folder (do not delete it first) and that picture stays.
Pictures for products you have deleted are not removed by extracting; clear them
with Admin Dashboard > Operations > "Delete unused pictures" when you are ready.

WHAT THE ORDER LOG STORES
- Reference, date, status, every line with SKU, variant, quantity, unit price
  and line total, the piece count, the order total, and the CUSTOMER'S NAME.
- It does NOT store the phone number, the business name, or the order notes.
  Those are typed into the cart, travel in the WhatsApp message, and never reach
  the server. Export CSV exports the same fields, name included.

SPREADSHEET IMPORT
- Export CSV from the dashboard, edit in Excel, and import the same file back.
- Import updates products by their product_id and adds any new rows. It does not
  delete: products you remove from the spreadsheet stay in the catalog until you
  delete them in the dashboard.

BULK PRODUCT PICTURES
- Name each main picture exactly as its SKU: DR-00001.jpg
- Add gallery pictures with a number: DR-00001-2.jpg, DR-00001-3.webp
- JPG, PNG and WEBP only, maximum 5 MB each. Upload in batches if your host
  limits one selection to 20 files.

SECURITY
- The customer passcode is the only thing between the public and your prices.
- Five wrong password attempts from one connection locks logins for 15 minutes.
- TURNING ON TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION IS STRONGLY RECOMMENDED —
  Account & store > two-factor.

NOTE ON PRICE TIERS
A single cart line is capped at 999 pieces, so a tier whose minimum quantity is
1000 or more can never be reached. Keep tier minimums below 1000.
